# Support & Community Resources 💬

Welcome to the CompanyOS community! Below are the recommended channels for obtaining assistance, proposing ideas, and interacting with maintainers and other users.

---

## ❓ Where to Seek Support

| Question / Issue Type | Recommended Channel |
| :--- | :--- |
| **How-To / Usage Questions** | [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/theunrealfusion/CompanyOS/discussions) |
| **Bug Reports** | [GitHub Issues (Bug Form)](https://github.com/theunrealfusion/CompanyOS/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) |
| **Feature Requests** | [GitHub Issues (Feature Form)](https://github.com/theunrealfusion/CompanyOS/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml) |
| **Security Concerns** | Email `security@companyos.ai` (See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)) |
| **Real-time Community Chat** | Join our Discord / Community Slack (links in README.md) |

---

## 💡 Best Practices for Support Requests

1. **Search Before Asking**: Check existing issues, discussions, and documentation (`docs/`) to see if your question has already been answered.
2. **Provide Reproducible Context**: When reporting bugs or unexpected behavior, include your OS, Python version, Node.js version, and relevant logs.
3. **Be Respectful**: Follow our [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) in all community interactions.
